# Product: Loyalty points

## The client's brief

We want our regular customers to come back. Every purchase earns points, and points can be
spent on a later purchase. Today we stamp paper cards and people lose them or forget them
at home.

## The story

Nuria Campos runs Verde, two organic food shops in Seville. Her regulars come two or three
times a week, and she wants to keep them now that a supermarket chain has opened an
organic aisle two streets away. Today every customer gets a paper card with ten boxes to
stamp. People lose the cards, leave them at home, or show up with three half-full ones. At
seven in the evening, with a queue to the door, the cashier is counting stamps by hand and
stamping twice to be kind.

## What we know about the business

- Verde has handed out about 1,800 paper cards. 4 regulars in 10 forget the card on any
  given day.
- The average basket is 23 euros. The busiest hour is seven to eight in the evening.
- Almost every regular has a mobile phone. Many of the older ones never open an app or a
  QR code.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Nuria's accountant needs it: unspent
  points count as a debt to the customer.
- Nuria can give away 5 % of a sale, not more, or the shop stops making money on it.

## The goal

Points live in both shops before the summer, when the regulars start to go to the beach.
Done, for Nuria, means a regular earns and spends points at the till without a card and
without slowing the queue.

## Open decisions

The group decides each one with the chief. The chief writes the decision below it.

### 1. How points are earned

1 point per euro, or 1 point per 10 euros but each point worth more? Per euro feels
generous and is easy to explain; per 10 euros keeps numbers small.

What we know: The average basket is 23 euros: 23 points, or 2.

**Decision:** _pending_

### 2. How the customer is identified

Phone number, or a QR code on their phone? A phone number works for everyone; a QR code is
faster and keeps the number private.

What we know: Almost everyone has a phone; many of the older regulars never open a QR
code.

**Decision:** _pending_

### 3. Minimum to spend points

Any amount, or blocks of 100 points? Any amount is flexible; blocks are easier at the
till.

What we know: The queue peaks at seven in the evening, and Nuria can give away 5 % of a
sale at most.

**Decision:** _pending_
