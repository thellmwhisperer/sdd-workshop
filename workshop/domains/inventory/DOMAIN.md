# Product: Stock across shops

## The client's brief

We have four shops in the same city. Each shop keeps its own stock in a spreadsheet. When
a customer wants a size that the shop does not have, the staff phone the other shops one
by one. We want one page where staff see the stock of every shop and can reserve units in
another shop for a customer.

## The story

Hélène Garnier runs Sabot, four shoe and clothing shops in Nantes. Each shop keeps its
stock in its own spreadsheet, updated at night if somebody remembers. A customer tries a
jacket in the old town shop and asks for it in size M. There is none on the shelf, so the
assistant starts phoning the other three shops. The first one does not pick up, the second
one has it but is busy, and by the time the third one calls back the customer has gone.
When a shop does put a unit aside for someone, it is a sticky note on a box in the back
room.

## What we know about the business

- Each shop makes about 9 of those calls a day, 4 minutes each. Last month 23 sales were
  lost waiting for an answer.
- 7 in 10 of the questions are about a size or a colour, not about the product.
- Of the boxes put aside with a sticky note, 3 in 10 are never collected and stay out of
  stock for weeks.
- 8 in 10 customers who reserve come within two days.
- Stock can never go below zero: a shop cannot reserve or sell a unit it does not have.
  Hélène's auditor counts it twice a year.
- Shop managers are on the shop floor two days a week; the rest of the time they are at
  the office.

## The goal

One page, open in all four shops, before the spring collection arrives in March. Done, for
Hélène, means that a customer asking for a size in another shop leaves with a reservation
instead of walking out.

## Open decisions

The group decides each one with the chief. The chief writes the decision below it.

### 1. Unit of stock

Count single products, or each product per size and colour? Per size is what customers ask
for; single products is much simpler to keep.

What we know: 7 in 10 questions are about a size or a colour.

### 2. How long a reservation lasts

Until the end of the day, or 48 hours? Short keeps stock free; long gives the customer
time to come.

### 3. Who can reserve
