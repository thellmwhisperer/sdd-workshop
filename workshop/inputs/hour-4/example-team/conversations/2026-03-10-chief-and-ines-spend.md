# Chief and Ines · 10 March

Ines: Finance sent the invoice for model usage in February. We spent three times the budget. Where did the money go?
Chief: I checked the cost logs. Most of the cost is me being woken. The watcher woke me every 5 minutes, day and night: 7,056 wakes since 16 February, across the first 40 runs.
Chief: In 92% of those wakes nothing had changed. Each wake reads the state of every run, about 9,000 tokens, to find nothing. We paid for tokens that did no work.
Ines: So we paid for the chief to look at a screen where nothing moved.
Chief: Yes. Proposal: no timer. The watcher wakes me only on an event: a run finished, the gate failed, a review is waiting for a person. That should cut the chief's share of the cost by more than 90%.
Ines: Do it. And put the wakes in the weekly costs so we see it next time.
