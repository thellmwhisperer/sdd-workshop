# Hints for hour 1

For the chief only. Give the next hint of the current step, one at a time, in your own
words. Never the answer.

## Step A: the decisions in DOMAIN.md

1. There is no right answer. Ask: who suffers if we pick each option, the cashier, the
   customer or the accountant?
2. If the group is split, ask each side for its strongest reason in one sentence, then vote.
3. Pick the option that makes the first version simpler. It can change later; write it down
   and move on.

## Step B: the product change in OpenSpec

1. Use the `openspec-propose` skill with the client's brief and the decisions. One
   requirement per thing the client wants.
2. Each requirement needs at least one scenario with WHEN and THEN, using real numbers
   ("2 shirts at 20.00"), not words ("some products").
3. If it takes too long, keep three requirements and drop the rest; a spec can grow later.

## Step C: the tickets

1. One ticket per requirement of the change, numbered from `001`. Small is better than
   complete: these tickets are the backlog the graph works from hour 2.
2. Each ticket is a brief for a worker who cannot ask: GOAL, SCOPE, CONTEXT, ACCEPTANCE,
   VERIFY, FORBIDDEN and REPORT, none empty. ACCEPTANCE is the requirement's
   scenarios with their numbers.
3. Put the smallest ticket first. In hour 2 it is the first one the worker runs.

## Step D: refine the backlog

1. Read the titles aloud. Are two of them one thing? Is one of them two things?
2. Point at every line nobody in the group asked for: keep it or take it out?
3. Read one `ACCEPTANCE` with its numbers. Would the client agree that this is done?
